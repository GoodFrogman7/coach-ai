# Footwork & Movement Fundamentals

## Common Question Phrasing

This section covers: How do I improve my footwork? What is a split step? When should I split step? How do I move better on court? What is split-step timing? How do I fix my split step? What is good footwork in tennis?

## Why Footwork Matters
Good footwork puts you in position to execute technique. Poor footwork forces compensations that lead to errors.

## Split Step

### What It Is
A small hop performed as your opponent contacts the ball. It loads your legs like springs for explosive movement.

### Timing
- Execute split step DURING opponent's forward swing
- Land as they make contact
- Feet shoulder-width apart
- Weight on balls of feet

### Common Errors
- Too early: Lands before opponent's contact → lose momentum
- Too late: After contact → slow reaction
- Too high: Wastes energy, slow recovery

## Recovery Time

### Definition
Time from your contact to return-to-ready position. Elite players: <1.5 seconds.

### Components
1. Deceleration after stroke
2. Change of direction
3. Return to center or tactical position
4. Split step preparation

### Improving Recovery
- Push off with outside leg (not hop)
- Keep center of mass low
- Use small adjustment steps, not big strides
- Anticipate based on your shot placement

## Lateral Movement

### Push-Off Technique
- Load outside leg
- Drive laterally with explosive push
- First step is long, subsequent steps shorter
- Stay low through the movement

### Balance
Maintain balance by:
- Wide base during stroke
- Core engagement
- Controlled deceleration
- Eyes level (no head bobbing)

## Drills
- Side-to-side shuffle with split step
- Shadow swings with recovery step
- Cone drills for lateral speed
- Balance board for stability training
