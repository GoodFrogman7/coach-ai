# Fatigue vs Technique Issues

## The Challenge
When performance drops, is it fatigue or technique regression? Understanding the difference guides your training decisions.

## Fatigue Signals

### Biomechanical Indicators
- **Recovery time increases**: Taking longer to return to ready position
- **Range of motion decreases**: Hip rotation, shoulder turn become limited
- **Variability increases**: Inconsistent stroke patterns
- **Balance instability**: More lateral drift, unsteady stance
- **Movement quality declines**: Slower footwork, less explosive

### Pattern Recognition
Fatigue typically affects:
1. Lower body first (legs, footwork)
2. Core stability
3. Fine motor control last (touch, feel)

## Technique Issues

### Biomechanical Indicators
- **Consistent errors**: Same mistake repeatedly
- **Phase-specific problems**: Issue isolated to one movement phase
- **Present when fresh**: Error occurs even at session start
- **Compensations visible**: Body adjusts incorrectly to execute stroke

### Pattern Recognition
Technique issues:
1. Appear regardless of fatigue level
2. Often phase-specific (e.g., poor preparation only)
3. Can be corrected with focus/awareness
4. May worsen under fatigue but root cause is technical

## How to Differentiate

### Ask These Questions
1. **Does the issue improve with rest?** → Fatigue
2. **Is it present from the first stroke?** → Technique
3. **Does it worsen progressively during session?** → Fatigue
4. **Is it consistent across multiple sessions?** → Technique
5. **Does conscious focus improve it immediately?** → Technique

### Mixed Cases
Often it's both:
- Fatigue exposes technique weaknesses
- Poor technique accelerates fatigue
- Address both for optimal improvement

## Training Implications

### If Fatigue-Driven
- Prioritize recovery
- Reduce training volume
- Focus on conditioning
- Monitor readiness before sessions

### If Technique-Driven
- Increase deliberate practice
- Slow-motion repetition
- Video analysis
- Drill-based correction

### If Mixed
- Address technique when fresh
- Build conditioning to maintain technique under fatigue
- Progressive overload: technique → volume → intensity

## Coach AI Context
Coach AI detects fatigue through:
- Recovery time trends
- Movement quality degradation
- Metric variability increases
- Session-to-session patterns

Use this alongside your self-awareness and coach feedback.
